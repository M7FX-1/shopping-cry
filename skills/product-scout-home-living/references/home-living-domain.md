it when a verdict needs the standard dimension tables, foam-density and

## Furniture material and emissions detail

- **Board emissions `[S]`**: CARB Phase 2 / US TSCA Title VI limits —
  hardwood plywood 0.05 ppm, particleboard 0.09 ppm, MDF 0.11 ppm
  formaldehyde; the European E1 grade is ≤0.1 ppm. Solid wood carries
  negligible added formaldehyde. "E0" is a stricter claim (Chinese
  standard lineage) — treat as marketing unless certified.
- **Wood honesty checklist `[P]`**: ask which parts are solid (frame?
  panels? legs?), look for veneer edge-banding photos, check drawer
  bottoms and backs — the hidden panels are where MDF hides.
- **Hardware hierarchy `[P]`**: ball-bearing or undermount metal
  runners > epoxy-coated metal > plastic; soft-close hinges from a
  nameable supplier > unbranded; corner blocks + dowels + cam-locks >
  staples alone.

## Upholstery and foam numbers

- **Martindale upholstery bands `[S]`**: <10,000 decorative;
  10,000–15,000 light domestic; 15,000–25,000 everyday family;
  25,000–30,000 heavy residential; 30,000+ contract. Wyzenbeek double
  rubs are a different test with different numbers — never compare
  across tests.
- **Seat foam `[P]`**: <25 kg/m³ flattens in 1–2 years; ≥30 kg/m³
  (high-resilience) is the daily-use floor; 35–40+ for heavy use.
  Back cushions are lighter-density by design — judge seat foam only.
- **Fiber notes**: solution-dyed acrylic/polyester blends resist sun
  fade for sunny rooms; cotton/linen upholstery looks right but
  wrinkles and stains — performance finishes are the trade-off; the
  clothing domain doc owns generic fiber science — cross-reference.

## Mattress detail

- **Foam density bands `[P]`**: <25 kg/m³ budget (flattens early);
  30–40 kg/m³ decent; 45–50+ kg/m³ premium memory/HR. Support cores
  run denser than comfort layers — ask for the core density.
- **Springs `[P]`**: Bonnell/continuous = budget, motion transfers;
  pocket = isolates motion, conforms; gauge: 12–13 thick/firmer wire,
  14–15 lighter. Spring count is marketing until gauge and zoning are
  stated.
- **Firmness matching `[P]`**: side sleepers → medium/medium-soft
  (shoulder sink); back/stomach → medium-firm to firm; heavier
  sleepers → firmer + higher density. Latex runs naturally firmer and
  cooler than memory foam; memory foam sleeps warmer — a hot-climate
  factor.
- **Fine print `[P]`**: sag warranties usually trigger at a dip depth
  (often 3–4 cm) and exclude handles/edging; a "10-year warranty" on
  <30 kg/m³ foam is not a durability claim.

## Standard dimension tables

- **Mattress sizes `[P]` — measure, don't trust the name** (Egyptian
  market approximations): single 90×190 cm; double 120×190–200;
  "queen" 160×190–200; "king" 180×200. Regional labels vary — the
  tape decides.
- **Circulation clearances `[P]`**: walkways ≥60 cm minimum
  (75–90 cm comfortable); bed-to-wall for making the bed ~60–70 cm;
  dining chair pull-back ~75 cm; coffee-table-to-sofa 40–45 cm.
- **Route check `[P]`**: measure doorways (typical Egyptian apartment
  door ~80–90 cm), stair turns, elevator cabin (many are ~80×100 cm —
  a 180 cm sofa needs rotation or hoisting); flat-pack cartons are
  often the binding dimension, not the furniture.

## Food-contact plastics — resin codes `[S]/[P]`

| Code | Polymer | Food use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 PET | Polyethylene terephthalate | Single-use bottles; not for reuse/heating |
| 2 HDPE | High-density polyethylene | Storage jugs, containers — safe, no heat |
| 4 LDPE | Low-density polyethylene | Bags, films, squeeze bottles — no heat |
| 5 PP | Polypropylene | The microwave-safe default (labeled ones) |
| 6 PS | Polystyrene | Foam/clarity containers — never heated |
| 7 Other | incl. PC (BPA legacy) | Avoid for food unless clearly modern Tritan/copolyester |

Heat, fat, and time drive migration `[P]` — glass/ceramic for
reheating is the universal safe answer; scratched/cloudy = replace.

## Lighting numbers

- **Lumen mappings `[S]`**: 60W incandescent ≈ 800 lm; 75W ≈ 1,100;
  100W ≈ 1,600. LED efficacy bands `[P]`: ≥80 lm/W decent,
  ≥100 lm/W good.
- **CCT**: 2700K warm (living/bedroom), 4000K neutral (kitchen/bath/
  work), 5000–6500K daylight (task). CRI ≥80 general, ≥90 color-
  critical.
- **Lifetime `[P]`**: 15,000–25,000 h normal band; dimmer
  compatibility must be stated on the box; visible flicker on camera
  = cheap driver, disqualify.

## Per-sub-domain driver detail `[P]`

- **Sofas**: Martindale band for the household's traffic; frame
  material; seat-foam density; route fit before model love; cushion
  construction (foam vs fiber-back trade-off: look vs recovery).
- **Wardrobes/storage**: board standard (E1/CARB P2), back-panel
  thickness, runner quality, anchoring rail (SKILL.md behavior 8) for
  anything tall.
- **Dining/kitchen tables**: solid tops resist heat/moisture better
  than veneered MDF; glass = easy wipe, shows smudges; check leg
  joinery — tables are levered daily.
- **Cookware**: match sets to actual cooking (a 3-piece quality set
  beats a 12-piece filler set); lid/knob quality is the cheap tell.
- **Bedding**: TC 200–400 `[P]`; percale for hot climates; Egyptian
  cotton seal per the clothing domain doc; pillow filling per sleep
  position (down-alternative washable default).
- **Towels**: 400–500 gsm everyday, 500–600 plush `[P]`; mid-GSM for
  humid bathrooms; combed/zero-twist cotton holds loops.
- **Storage/organization**: load ratings on shelves are marketing
  unless tested; prefer steel/PP over thin acrylic for heavy items;
  measure before modular systems.
- **Prayer rugs**: wool/cotton vs microfiber per sweat tolerance;
  pile height for prostration; anti-slip base; edge/fringe density.
- **Quran printings**: recognized publisher + standard Uthmani
  edition; sewn binding for daily use; paper gsm to reduce
  see-through; size per reader (large print for elders).
