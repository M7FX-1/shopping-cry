# Sports-outdoors — domain reference (demoted detail; no operative safety rule lives here)

Every operative rail (helmet co-purchase, certification gates,
crashed-helmet retirement, glove weight classes, ISO 12402 levels,
heat-escalation wording) lives in the SKILL.md main file. This
reference holds the charts and worked math the main file points at.
Verification tags: `[S]` = sourced — verified against a regulator, standards body, official/primary source, or a family-verified precedent this cycle (inline `[S: URL]` carries the source); `[P]` = practitioner/market consensus or street anchor — labeled as such, never presented as sourced fact; `[A]` = anchored rail — a calibrated threshold set at install and re-anchored on a ~6-month cadence. Suffixes in any order: a source number (`[S13]`), a `YYYY-MM` date (mandatory on `[A]`), and lowercase modifiers — `unverified` (secondary coverage only; re-verify or drop before stating as fact), `industry` (vendor/marketing source), `convention` (usage convention, not evidence). A `[S, unverified]` claim is never the sole basis for a dosage or contraindication statement. If a
rule here ever disagrees with the SKILL.md, the SKILL.md wins.

## Helmet standards decode `[S]`

| Standard | Jurisdiction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CPSC 16 CFR 1203 | US | mandatory since March 1999; impact attenuation, retention-system strength, positional stability — the stringent baseline |
| CE EN 1078 (+A1) | EU | bikes + skateboards/roller skates; self-certified under CE; generally less stringent than CPSC 1203 |
| AS/NZS 2063 | AU/NZ | mandatory in Australia/New Zealand |
| Snell B-90/B-95 | voluntary (US-class) | above-baseline voluntary testing |

Sources: eCFR 16 CFR 1203; CPSC bicycle-helmet guidance; EN 1078
explainers `[S]`. Fit basics (level, snug straps, no rock) are
practitioner `[P]`. Crashed-helmet retirement is standard-safety
consensus `[P]`.

## Team-sport ball size chart `[S]`

| Size | Circumference | Weight | Age/level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | ~58–61 cm | ~350 g | ~U5–U8 |
| 4 | ~63–66 cm | ~350–390 g | ~U8–U12 |
| 5 (official match ball, IFAB Law 2) | 68–70 cm | 410–450 g | 13+ / official play |

IFAB Law 2 defines size 5 officially; sizes 3–4 are youth
federation/league recommendations. Inflation printed at the valve —
size 5 ≈ 0.6–1.1 bar (8.5–15.6 psi) `[P]`. Basketball (FIBA): size 7
men / size 6 women-youth; handball: size 3 men / size 2 women-youth
`[P]` (federation class conventions).

## Sleeping-bag ratings decode (EN 13537 → ISO 23537-1) `[S]`

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Upper Limit | highest comfortable temperature (bag partly open) |
| Comfort | standard woman sleeps comfortably through the night |
| Lower Limit | standard man, 8 h sleep without waking from cold |
| Extreme | 6-h survival threshold — never a shopping target |

Shop by Comfort (cold sleepers) / Lower Limit (warm sleepers) against
the actual expected nighttime low (desert and mountain nights drop
sharply after sunset). Tested bags exclude extreme-expedition ranges
(limit < −24 °C). Sources: REI guides, Mountain Equipment explainer,
ISO 23537-1:2022 `[S]`.

## Life-jacket levels (ISO 12402) `[S]`

| Level | Newtons | Class |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | 50N | buoyancy aid — swimmers, near help; does NOT turn unconscious wearer |
| 70 | 70N | flotation aid (US/CA harmonized class) |
| 100 | 100N | lifejacket — turns most wearers face-up; sheltered/coastal |
| 150 / 275 | 150N+ | offshore/professional |

US/CA equivalent: US Coast Guard / Transport Canada approval system;
AU equivalent: AS 4758. Inflatable armbands/water wings = toys per
CPSC — never flotation devices.

## Bike sizing math `[P]`

- Road frame (cm) ≈ inseam (cm) × 0.65; MTB frame (in) ≈ inseam ×
  0.66 ÷ 2.54, then prefer the smaller when between sizes.
- Standover clearance: road 1–2 in; MTB 2–4 in. Reach and saddle
  height outrank the formula at the margins — a test sit wins.
- Kids' bikes by wheel diameter: 12 in (~2–4 y), 16 (~4–6 y), 20
  (~6–9 y), 24 (~9–12 y) — size by the child's height, not age
  `[P]`; the clothing kids rule (height over age) applies.
- Home gym payback worked example `[P]` (illustrative in USD — compute
  in the user's local currency): machine $750 vs gym $50/month →
  payback at 15 months of REAL use; idle-by-March reality makes the
  gym cheaper for year-one starters. Adherence honesty ships with the
  math.

## Yoga mat thickness bands `[P]`

| Thickness | Use |
|---|---|
| 3–6 mm | balance-sensitive practice, travel — the standard choice |
| 8–12 mm | joint comfort, floor exercises, home stretching |
| > 12 mm | knee/cushion comfort only — wobbles balance poses |

Non-slip surface and material smell (open-cell vs closed-cell) are
the quality axes.

## Channel & authenticity guidance `[P]`

- Verify established sporting-goods chains, authorized brand dealers,
  and reputable marketplaces serving the user's country at dispatch.
- Second-hand marketplaces: acceptable for weights/tents/racks;
  conditional for bikes/rackets; never for helmets or climbing ropes
  (SKILL behavior 1).
- Replica-shirt honesty: replicas sold AS replicas are honest products;
  fakes sold as originals are the trap — the distinction is stated in
  the verdict, not assumed.

## Heat-training rails detail `[P]`

- Timing: early morning / evening; midday extreme-heat outdoor
  sessions are the avoid default.
- Hydration: planned before/during/after — thirst is a late signal.
- Stop signs: dizziness, nausea, confusion, sweating cessation →
  stop, cool, shade; severe signs escalate per the health-medical
  skill's emergency framing (the shopping skill never manages
  heatstroke — route).
- Gear interaction: dark kit absorbs heat; mesh/ventilated picks and
  the clothing skill's wicking-fiber logic apply to what is worn,
  not what is bought here.
