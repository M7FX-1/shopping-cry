# Health & Medical — Drivers, Specs, and Kit Detail

This is the demoted-companion reference for the product-scout-health-
medical skill (installed at `references/health-drivers.md`, alongside
`references/health-domain.md` which holds the full sourced domain
knowledge). Read it when a shopping-guidance question needs the
per-sub-domain drivers, or when numeric sizing/osmolality specs or kit
organization detail is useful.

**Nothing operative lives here**: every safety threshold, gate, and

## Sub-domain decision drivers (product-shopping requests)

- **Pain/fever**: paracetamol first-line; ibuprofen when inflammation
  matters (dental, musculoskeletal, period pain) and no GI/kidney/
  pregnancy/anticoagulant gate (skip-cases: diarrhea/dehydration and
  pre-surgery — see SKILL.md behavior 5); the aspirin ranking rule is
  operative in SKILL.md behavior 5 (never a shopping pick; Reye/
  GI-bleeding reasons sourced in the domain file).
- **Cough/cold/allergy**: one active per symptom (2nd-generation
  antihistamines are first-line — SKILL.md behavior 2); cetirizine is
  slightly more sedating than loratadine/fexofenadine in some people;
  honey ≥1 y + saline/humidity/fluids as the honest cough plan; spray
  congestion >3 days → doctor (rebound).
- **Heartburn**: antacid (immediate) vs H2 (14-day course) vs PPI
  (frequent heartburn, course rules in SKILL.md behavior 5); red
  flags → referral.
- **Supplements**: goal → evidence tier first; athletes → NSF-CFS/
  Informed Sport only; iron/vitamin D framed test-first, not
  shelf-first; UL screen on every multi-nutrient product (SKILL.md
  behavior 7).
- **Devices**: validated-list membership first (STRIDE BP/ValidateBP,
  ISO 15197:2013 — SKILL.md behavior 8); cuff size; consumable
  availability (cuffs, strips, batteries); thermometer type by
  household age; one device used consistently.
- **Sexual wellness**: condom nominal-width match before brand;
  lubricant osmolality/pH before marketing; EC timeline honesty;
  fertility-awareness expectations set numerically (~93% typical-use
  for the only FDA-cleared app).
- **First aid kit**: household profile → contents (ages, severe
  allergy → epinephrine auto-injector first-line, chronic disease,
  travel); nothing from the SKILL.md NOT-to-keep list.
- **Massage/relaxation**: judged as consumer devices (safety
  certification, intensity control, stated intended use);
  contraindications (acute injury phase, suspected DVT, pregnancy →
  professional advice first) are operative in SKILL.md behavior 8;
  evidence for wellness claims is thin — say so rather than repeating
  marketing.

## Sexual wellness numeric specs

- **Condom sizing (nominal width = flat width, mm)**: snug ~47–49,
  standard ~52–56, XL ~56–60. `[P]` A wrong size = breakage/slippage.
- **Lubricant quality thresholds (WHO)**: vaginal lubricants
  osmolality <1,200 mOsm/kg and pH ≈4.5; anal pH 5.5–7. Most
  commercial water-based lubes run 2,000–6,000 mOsm/kg (glycerin/
  propylene glycol) — hyperosmolar, mucosa-damaging. Polyurethane
  condoms transmit heat and work with oil-based lubricants — check
  the brand's instructions `[S, unverified`.
- Reformulated iso-osmolar, pH-matched compliant products exist —
  prefer them when available in-market.

## First-aid kit organization detail

Single labeled box; alphabetical or by-use trays; every item dated on
purchase; quarterly expiry check; restock after every use; device
items (BP monitor, thermometer, spacer) stored away from heat; use
the pharmacist's discard program for expired medicines `[P, unverified]`.

## Practical extras (sourced in health-domain.md)

- **Paracetamol Extra-Strength labels self-limit to 3,000 mg/day**
  after FDA's 2011 push — some products carry a stricter label cap
  than the 4,000 mg ceiling; the package insert is the authority
  (SKILL.md behavior 5).
- **Infant formula preparation (variable tap-water quality)**: boiled
  then cooled water for infant-prep contexts `[S, unverified`.
- **Kit heat exposure**: summer storage heat degrades medicines and
  condoms — never leave kits in cars (echo of SKILL.md behavior 11,
  repeated here because kit-building is when storage gets decided).
