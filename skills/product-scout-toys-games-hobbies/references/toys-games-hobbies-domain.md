## Toy-safety standards decode `[S]`

| Standard / mark | Jurisdiction | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| ASTM F963-23 (mandatory via 16 CFR 1250, CPSC rule effective 2024) | US | toys for ≤12 (battery-accessibility parts extend to 14): mechanical, flammability, chemicals, battery access |
| 16 CFR 1501 small-parts cylinder | US | choke-test cylinder — parts that fit = fails for <3 toys; drives 3–6 choking warnings |
| EN 71-1/-2/-3 under the Toy Safety Directive (CE) | EU | -1 mechanical/physical, -2 flammability, -3 chemical migration |
| ASTM D-4236 (LHAMA, CPSC-enforced) | US | art materials: toxicologist-reviewed formula; hazards DISCLOSED on label — not "non-toxic" |
| 16 CFR 1263 (Reese's Law standard, 2023) | US | button-cell/coin batteries: child-resistant compartments + warnings |
| ISO 8124 | international | toy safety series (basis for many national adoptions) |

Key sources: CPSC toy-safety business guidance; Federal Register
final rule Jan 2024 (F963-23); CPSC Reese's Law release Sep 2023;
CPSC art-materials guidance; ACMI regulations page.

## Button-battery emergency framing `[S]`

- CPSC: swallowed coin cells burn through esophagus tissue in as
  little as 2 hours; injuries continue after removal; documented
  deaths 2011–2021 motivated the rule.
- Wording carried verbatim in SKILL behavior 2 — the shopping-side
  rail; medical follow-up belongs to the health-medical skill's
  emergency framing (its red-flag screen).
- Also applies to: remote controls, musical cards, light-up
  accessories, fidget gadgets — name once when present in a verdict.

## Magnet-toy rules `[S]`

- CPSC has taken regulatory action against high-powered magnet sets
  (small neodymium balls); ingestion injuries are surgical-emergency
  class; multiple magnets are worse than one.
- Designed-play magnet toys (tiles, fishing games) are acceptable at
  matched age labels; the ingestion emergency wording applies to any
  magnet.

## Counterfeit building-set checks `[P]` (with brand-official basis)

| Check | Genuine tell | Fake tell |
|---|---|---|
| Molded stud logo | LEGO on most studs (cylinders/cones exempt) | missing, or odd surface ridges |
| Box print | crisp, aligned, manufacturer address | blurry, misspellings ("LELE/LEQI-class"), no address |
| Clutch power | firm, consistent | loose/wobbly, uneven |
| Material | consistent ABS gloss/color | washed-out color, flash marks, brittle feel |
| Minifigs | snug leg/torso fits | loose fits, soft detail |
| Set math | part count matches claim | extra/missing pieces reported |

LEGO runs official anti-counterfeit actions; alternatives sold under
their own brands are legal products — judge on quality, age label,
and clutch power.

## Gift fast-path worked examples `[P]`

- Age 4, ~$15 equivalent, loves vehicles → one-line pick (a die-cast/
  classic set), cheaper alternate (a multipack), sibling-safer
  alternate (no small parts), age-label line, durability note.
- Age 10, ~$35 equivalent, wants "science" → kit that builds/
  experiments over a screen-gadget; check D-4236/CE/ASTM marks;
  compartment gate for any cells.
- Budget can't buy decent at the age (e.g., ~$3 for a 7-year-old
  builder) → say it, give best-of-bracket clearly labeled, or move to
  books/board-game class where the budget lands honestly.

## Counterfeit & channel guidance `[S]` / `[P]`

- Global counterfeit-toy context `[S]`: EU/OLAF operations seized
  7.8M+ counterfeit/dangerous toys (Nov 2025); industry figure ~75%
  of seized counterfeit toys fail safety tests.
- Channels (verify at dispatch for the user's market): prefer
  established national toy retailers, official brand stores, and
  verified retailers over unvetted third-party marketplace sellers;
  apply inspection checks to local second-hand listings.
- Fireworks refusal stands globally regardless of local legality.

## Student-instrument grading detail `[P]`

| Grade | Reality | Buy logic |
|---|---|---|
| Student | plywood/composite bodies, laminate finishes | tunability + setup quality > tonewood words; included gig bag; rent-to-own when commitment unproven |
| Intermediate | better hardware, some solid-wood parts | only after the child outgrows the student instrument |
| Professional | tonewood, hand-setup, provenance | dealer/pedagogue verification; the skill verifies seller + return path only |

Instrument classes in scope: recorder/ukulele (entry), keyboard,
guitar, violin, drums/percussion kits. Audio electronics (amps,
interfaces) → electronics skill.
